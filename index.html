About ROOMZY

ROOMZY is India's trusted platform for students to find verified PGs, hostels, and co-living spaces. We simplify the search for comfortable, safe, and affordable accommodation near colleges and workplaces.

Our Mission

To make student housing transparent, safe, and hassle-free by connecting students with verified property owners.

What We Offer

  • 50,000+ happy students served
  • 2,500+ verified PGs across major cities
  • 100% verified listings with real photos
  • 24/7 customer support
  • Secure booking and payment

Our Values

  • Trust: Every property is verified before listing
  • Transparency: Real photos, real reviews, real prices
  • Safety: Background checks and secure transactions
  • Support: Dedicated help for students and owners

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Booking Cancellations

  • 30+ days before check-in: 100% refund (minus processing fee)
  • 15-29 days before check-in: 75% refund
  • 7-14 days before check-in: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days: No refund (owner's discretion)

Refund Process

  • Initiate cancellation from "My Bookings"
  • Refunds processed within 7-10 business days
  • Refunded to original payment method

Special Cases

  • Medical emergencies: Case-by-case review with documentation
  • Property misrepresentation: Full refund + assistance finding alternative
  • Owner cancellation: Full refund + 10% compensation

Non-Refundable

  • Processing fees and taxes
  • No-shows or early departure
  • Violation of house rules

For disputes: refunds@roomzy.in
